We’re rebuilding FREAK VAULT

Something broke, and we’re not going to dress it up. Our database server was hit by a serious incident, and the damage ran deeper than a quick patch could fix.

Here’s what we know so far:

  • 16+ drum kits leaked
  • 5 preset packs leaked
  • 946 lines of CSS exposed
  • 99+ bugs found across the site

The database itself and some core parts of the system were hit too. Patching over that would only hide the problem, so we’re taking the damaged parts apart and rebuilding them properly.

Right now we’re:

  • Rebuilding the database from the ground up
  • Fixing every one of those bugs
  • Going through the site’s internals and locking them down
  • Bringing back the assets that were affected

Until we’re done, expect things to be missing or act up. Thanks for sticking with us. We’re not quitting, we’re just making sure it’s built right this time.

— Freak.
